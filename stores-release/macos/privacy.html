Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 7, 2026

MCPOrb ("we", "our") operates the MCPOrb Runner desktop application and the mcporb.ai website. This Privacy Policy explains what data we handle, how we use it, and your rights.

1. Data We Collect

MCPOrb Runner (desktop app) processes all data locally on your device. We do not collect, transmit, or store any personal data from the app. Specifically:

  • Orb files you import remain on your local disk.
  • Search queries and results stay on your device.
  • MCP configurations are written to local config files on your machine only.
  • No analytics, telemetry, or usage data is transmitted.

mcporb.ai website does not use tracking cookies or analytics. The contact form on the Design Partner Program section sends your provided information (name, company, email) directly to our team. We use this information solely to respond to your inquiry.

2. How We Use Your Information

If you contact us through the website, we use your information only to:

  • Respond to your inquiry or assessment request.
  • Provide requested services or support.

We do not sell, share, or use your information for marketing purposes.

3. Data Storage and Security

MCPOrb Runner stores application settings and Orb data in your local application support directory (~/Library/Application Support/MCPOrb/ on macOS). This data never leaves your device unless you explicitly share it.

Password-protected Orbs are encrypted using industry-standard encryption (ChaCha20-Poly1305 with Argon2 key derivation). Passwords are stored in the OS keychain and are never transmitted.

4. Third-Party Services

MCPOrb Runner does not connect to any external servers by default. The optional Orb Store feature allows you to download Orbs from mcporb.ai — this is a user-initiated action and only downloads the selected Orb file.

5. Children's Privacy

MCPOrb is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.

7. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at privacy@mcporb.ai.
