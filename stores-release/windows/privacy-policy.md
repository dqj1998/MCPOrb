# Privacy Policy

**Last Updated:** 2026-01-01

## Introduction

MCPOrb ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how MCPOrb Runner collects, uses, and safeguards information when you use our application.

## Information We Collect

### Local Processing Only

MCPOrb Runner processes all documents **entirely on your local device**. Your documents and generated packages never leave your machine during the build process.

### We Do Not Collect

- Personal identification information
- Document content or files
- Usage analytics or telemetry
- Device identifiers
- Location data

## Data Storage

### Local Storage

- **Documents** — Temporarily processed in memory; not stored permanently
- **Packages** — Saved only to locations you specify on your device
- **Settings** — Stored locally in your user profile

### No Cloud Storage

MCPOrb Runner does not upload your data to any cloud services or external servers.

## Third-Party Services

### Internet Connectivity

MCPOrb Runner may connect to the internet for:
- Downloading MCP runtime components (when enabled)
- Checking for updates (when enabled)

These connections do not transmit your document content.

### No Analytics

We do not use analytics services, tracking pixels, or data collection tools.

## Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or share your information with third parties. Your documents and packages remain under your complete control.

## Security

- All processing occurs locally on your device
- No data transmission to external servers during core functionality
- Orb files are processed in memory only

## Children's Privacy

MCPOrb Runner is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by updating the "Last Updated" date.

## Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us:

- **Website:** https://mcporb.ai
- **Support:** https://mcporb.ai/support

This Privacy Policy applies to MCPOrb Runner version 1.2.0 and later.
